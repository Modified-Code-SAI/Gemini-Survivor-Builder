## 2. 3D Character Rigging & Optical MoCap Invariants

- **Real Optical MoCap Only:** All playable survivor, raider, and Hollow Stalker locomotion, combat, and jump clips are baked from real optical motion capture (`C:\GameDev\assets\inbox\`) via our Blender AAA retargeting scripts (`C:\GameDev\assets\library\...`). **Never replace character animation with procedural sine-wave puppets.**
- **Collinear Swing-Twist Decomposition:** Twist bones (`upperarm_twist`, `forearm_twist`) MUST be solved using exact collinear swing-twist decomposition (`swing_only = swing(u0, new_shaft); axial_twist = full_w @ swing_only^-1`) paired with full 3×3 orthonormal palm/arm basis alignment to eliminate mid-bicep and mid-forearm S-kinks.
- **Articulated Hands & Split-Body Masking:** `survivor.glb` uses a 34-bone skeleton with 2-joint fist-curl fingers (`fingers_01_L/R`, `fingers_02_L/R`) and 18 baked clips. Load-time lower-body / upper-body clip splitting in `src/player/controller.ts` blends upper-body tool swings (`axe_swing`, `pickaxe_swing`), bow shots (`bow_shoot`), punches, and bottle throws seamlessly over lower-body running/jogging/walking with zero leg freezing or foot sliding.
- **Canonical Upright Tool Grip & Strict Tool-vs-Fist Routing:** All right-hand tools (`felling_axe.glb`, `pickaxe.glb`, `repair_hammer.glb`, and procedural fallbacks) MUST conform to canonical local axes (`origin=(0,0,0)` at the leather grip, `+Y` shaft upright, `-Z` cutting blade / front pick spike / waffle bell) and mount to `hand_R` via calibrated `HAND_TOOL_GRIP_QUATERNION` + `HAND_TOOL_GRIP_OFFSET`. Combat clip selection MUST strictly separate tools/weapons from fists (`resolveAttackClipKind`): `'axe'` -> `axeSwingAction ?? pickaxeSwingAction`, `'pickaxe'`/`'hammer'` -> `pickaxeSwingAction ?? axeSwingAction`, `'bow'` -> `bowShootAction`, and bare-fist punches (`attackRightAction` / `attackLeftAction`) are gated exclusively to `'fists'` — tool/bow actions MUST NEVER fall back to bare-fist punch clips.
- **Dual `hand_L` Compound Bow Carry-to-Aim Quaternion Slerp:** Because `hand_L` rests tilted beside the torso during idle/run clips and raises into firing orientation during `bow_shoot`, a single fixed quaternion either clips the bowstring through the forearm/bedroll during idle or cants the limbs off-vertical during draw. Always mount `compound_bow.glb` (`origin=(0,0,0)` grip, `+Y` vertical, `-Z` forward riser, `+Z` bowstring) to `hand_L` with `BOW_GRIP_OFFSET` and slerp smoothly from `BOW_CARRY_QUATERNION` to `BOW_AIM_QUATERNION` driven by `wBowShoot` (`leftBow.setAimBlend(wBowShoot * 1.4)` using pre-allocated quaternions, zero allocations, verified `30°–65°` arc in `controller.test.ts`).
- **GLB Bone Attachments:** Always detach reusable props (`chestFlashlight` on `spine_03`, `handTools` on `hand_R`, `leftBow` on `hand_L`) from the procedural fallback rig before disposing the fallback rig, compensate parent bone world scale cleanly (`invScale = 1 / boneWorldScale.x`), and dispose each prop **once** in `player.dispose()`.

## 3. Three.js r174 WebGL2 Rendering & Shader Invariants

- **GTAO Exclusion for Transparent / Additive Effects:** `GTAOPass` renders its depth/normal pass across scene meshes; transparent, additive, or `depthWrite: false` objects (campfire flames, rising sparks, build ghost previews, wood chips/stone shards, splinter/repair bursts, flashlight volumetric cones, chest lens glow, fireflies, scavenge glints, Stalker eye embers, status sprites, fallback vehicle glass, utility-pole wires) MUST set `mesh.userData['excludeFromGtao'] = true` so they never cast dark halo artifacts.
- **GLSL `pow()` NaN Guard (ANGLE / WebGL2):** Passing a slightly negative base into `pow(x, y)` in GLSL yields `NaN`, which blooms into permanent black square tiles inside `UnrealBloomPass`. Always clamp every `pow()` base with `max(0.0, x)` or `clamp(x, 0.0, 1.0)`.
- **Singularity-Free Sky Zenith:** Never use `atan(dir.x, dir.z)` directly at the zenith (`dir.y -> 1`) in starfield/sky shaders; use stereographic/projected coordinates `(dir.xz / (dir.y + 0.35)) * 125.0`.
- **Monotonic Rain Phase Integration:** Never multiply raw `uTime` by a changing fall-speed uniform inside the rain vertex shader (decreasing speed causes raindrops to fly upward!). Always integrate `uFallPhase` and `uWindPhase` monotonically on CPU (`dt`-accumulated).
- **Screen-Space Volumetric Sun Shafts:** Keep the HDR screen-space `VOLUMETRIC_SUN_SHADER` pipeline; never revert to static 2D god-ray billboard planes.
- **Reflective Puddle Atmosphere Sync:** All 7 rainwater puddles (2 foreground `Reflector` planar mirrors + 5 distant Fresnel puddles) must stay synced to `AtmosphereSnapshot` sky/fog/night tints so puddles never glow sunset-orange at midnight.
- **Single-Owner Texture Atlas Disposal:** `getWorldTextureAtlas()` owns all shared PBR `CanvasTexture` instances. Feature systems (`camp.ts`, `harvest.ts`, `building.ts`, `threats.ts`, `scavenge.ts`, `controller.ts`) dispose ONLY their own created `BufferGeometry` and `Material` objects — **never** dispose borrowed atlas textures.
- **Full-Forest Instanced Chopping (Zero Surveyor Bands):** Never spawn separate marked timber trees or tan surveyor bands (`blazeRingGeo`). Every playable valley forest tree across all 5 species (`|x| <= 54, z in [-88, 34]`) is choppable directly out of its shared GPU `InstancedMesh` batch via `FrontierEnvironment.forest` (`ChoppableForestController`), animating per-hit recoil shake, a 4-slot pre-allocated species-accurate `YXZ` falling-tree pool, and an instanced cut wood stump batch (`r in [0.28, 0.42] < 0.55`) with zero extra standing draw calls.

## 4. Gameplay, Physics, Performance & Browser Invariants

- **Zero Per-Frame Heap Allocations:** Never allocate `new THREE.Vector3()`, `Quaternion`, `Matrix4`, `Color`, or transient return objects inside `update()`, `updateCamera()`, `render()`, `frame()`, or high-frequency HUD queries (e.g., `building.getInteractTarget` reuses `scratchInteractTarget`, `building.getRepairTarget` reuses `scratchRepairTarget`, `camp.getNearestCampfire` writes into `scratchBaseAnchor`, and `threats.getSiegeStatus` writes into `scratchSiegeStatus`).
- **Complete Multi-Story Vertical Isolation Invariant:** Multi-story fortifications (`stairs`, `upper_floor`, `window_wall`, stacked `doorway`s/walls) rely on consistent vertical gating across EVERY spatial query so 1st- and 2nd-story actors never leak collisions, sightlines, prompts, traps, or melee swings across floor decks:
  1. **Height-Banded Colliders (`src/world/terrain.ts`):** All built wall/doorway/workbench/storage/spike OBBs carry explicit `minY`/`maxY` bounds; `resolveHorizontalCollision(..., actorY)` skips obstacles outside `[actorY + 0.16, actorY + actorHeight]`.
  2. **Elevation-Aware LOS (`src/systems/stealth.ts`):** `hasLineOfSight(..., eyeY)` skips height-banded obstacles outside `eyeY`, so 2nd-story walls/closed doors above an open 1st-story doorway never blind ground-floor AI (`eyeY = actor.y + 1.55`).
  3. **Story-Scoped Building Queries (`src/systems/building.ts`):** `getFloorHeightAt(..., currentY)` prevents jumping straight from 1st floor onto an overhead deck; `isInsideBuiltShelter(..., actorY)` checks roofs/decks above `actorY + 0.8`; `getInteractTarget(..., playerY)` restricts prompts/doors/workbenches/crates to `|p.y - playerY| <= 1.8`; `checkSpikeHit(..., actorY)` restricts traps to `|p.y - actorY| <= 1.6`; and `findNearestAttackableStructure(..., actorY)` filters **all** piece kinds against vertical melee reach (`p.y <= refY + 1.8` and `p.y + centerOffsetY >= actorY - 0.6`).
  4. **Vertical Melee Isolation (`src/systems/threats.ts`):** Both enemy melee initiation/hit delivery in `threats.update` and `tryPlayerMeleeHit(..., playerY)` strictly require `|playerY - actor.y| <= 1.75`, guaranteeing ground-floor Raiders/Stalkers beneath a 2nd-story watchtower bash the ground-floor walls instead of striking through the ceiling.
- **Line-of-Sight vs. Low Colliders:** `stealth.ts` treats circular obstacles with `radius >= MIN_OCCLUDING_CIRCLE_RADIUS (0.55)` as opaque sightline pillars. Keep low ground obstacles (campfire rings `r = 0.54`, instanced cut tree stumps `r in [0.28, 0.42] <= 0.42`) strictly `< 0.55` so enemies do not lose LOS behind ankle-high stones or stumps.
- **Spatial Placement Clearances:** All rock outcrops (`HARVEST_ROCK_SPECS`) maintain `>= 2.6 m` clearance from `HERO_TREE_COORDS`, foreground Red Alder `(-6.9, 9.8)`, landmarks, vehicles, boulders, and scavenge caches, and `>= 4.4 m` from `getRoadCenterX(z)`; `environment.ts` enforces a `2.8 m` radial exclusion around every rock outcrop during procedural forest placement, and exposes every playable forest tree inside `|x| <= 54, z in [-88, 34]` (covering `WORLD_BOUNDS + HARVEST_REACH`) as both collidable and choppable.
- **Arbitrary-Azimuth Tree Toppling:** Falling-tree pool pivots use `rotation.order = 'YXZ'` and `rotation.set(fallAngle, fallYaw, 0)` so diagonal falls tilt cleanly in a single vertical plane away from the survivor with zero roll skew.
- **Unified Support Height & Dry Decks:** `getSupportHeight(x, z, currentY?)` (`max(getTerrainHeight, building.getFloorHeightAt)`) is shared by the player controller, Raiders, Hollow Stalker, and thrown bottles; player puddle-footstep audio and chill checks mask puddles whenever `building.getFloorHeightAt(x, z, player.position.y) !== null`.
- **Hysteretic Campfire Repellent:** The Hollow Stalker uses dedicated `fireFleeTimer (1.6 s)` and `fireRecoilCooldown (6.5 s)` (separate from flashlight `recoilCooldown`) so it bounds cleanly away into the pines instead of vibrating on the `7.8 m` campfire perimeter.
- **Placement Validation & Duplicate-Click Guard:** `tryPlaceCurrent` always re-validates live constraints and immediately invalidates `ghostValid` to prevent duplicate-click stacking.
- **Chrome Pointer-Lock Hardening:** `controller.ts` gates `onPointerLockChange(false)` behind `wasPointerLocked` so Chrome's pointer-lock cooldown or focus bounce never slams `#start-overlay` back up; drag-to-look (`buttons & 1 || buttons & 2`), unlocked short-click (`< 25 px²`) Build Mode placement, and `[F]` / `[Enter]` placement ensure smooth play even without pointer lock.
- **Damaged Structure Fractional HP Serialization & Tamper-Resistant Persistence:** `building.serialize()` clamps damaged positive HP via `Math.max(1, Math.round(p.hp))` so sub-`0.5` fractional remaining HP never serializes as `0` and gets discarded on load. `persistence.ts` validates all fields via strict `unknown` narrowing (zero `any`), preserves backward compatibility with Phase 1 v1 saves by defaulting missing optional Phase 2 fields (`hp`, `arrows`, `gear`), clamps `player.x`/`player.z` inside `WORLD_BOUNDS`, and caps all loaded arrays (`builtPieces <= 96`, `depletedHarvestIds <= 320`, `lootedCacheIds <= 32`, `campfires/shelters <= 16`).
